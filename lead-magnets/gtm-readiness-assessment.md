## Scoring & Assessment

**Total Score: ___/150**

### Score Interpretation:

**120-150 (GTM Advanced)**
Your GTM strategy is well-developed. Focus on optimization and scaling what's working.

**90-119 (GTM Developing)**
You have solid foundations. Prioritize systematizing and improving your weakest areas.

**60-89 (GTM Basic)**
You have some components in place but need strategic focus. Start with target market clarity and systematic outbound.

**30-59 (GTM Emerging)**
Significant GTM development needed. Begin with defining your ideal client profile and value proposition.

**Below 30 (GTM Foundational)**
Start with the basics: ideal client definition, core messaging, and one systematic client acquisition channel.

## Next Steps Recommendations

### If your lowest score is Target Market Definition:
1. Define your ideal client profile using firmographic and behavioral criteria
2. Interview your best current clients to understand commonalities
3. Create detailed buyer personas for decision makers and influencers

### If your lowest score is Value Proposition:
1. Develop a one-sentence transformation statement
2. Gather proof points and case studies
3. Test messaging with current clients and prospects

### If your lowest score is Go-to-Market System:
1. Document your current client acquisition process
2. Identify and eliminate bottlenecks and gaps
3. Create systematic approaches for lead generation

### If your lowest score is Outbound Engine:
1. Build targeted prospect lists using defined criteria
2. Set up proper email infrastructure and tracking
3. Develop and test outreach templates and sequences

### If your lowest score is Content & Authority:
1. Create a content calendar aligned with client journey
2. Develop thought leadership pieces showcasing expertise
3. Build content distribution and engagement strategy

### If your lowest score is Revenue Operations:
1. Implement or optimize CRM system and processes
2. Define lead stages, qualification criteria, and routing rules
3. Build reporting dashboards for key metrics
