## General Rules

- **Word count:** 70-90 words per email (short and scannable)
- **Tone:** Conversational, peer-to-peer. Write like you're texting a colleague.
- **CTAs:** Soft asks only ("Interested?" or "Worth a look?" — never "Book a demo" or "Schedule a call")
- **Subject lines:** 3-5 words, all lowercase, no punctuation. Should look like an internal email.
- **Format:** Plain text only. No HTML styling, no bold, no images, no links in body.
- **One job per email:** Each email makes one point and one ask.

## Adaptation Notes

When writing copy for a specific campaign:
1. Replace template placeholders with actual content relevant to the campaign topic
2. Match the persona — use language that resonates with the target titles
3. Keep the tone consistent across all emails in the sequence
4. Test subject lines by reading them as if they appeared in your own inbox
